# M15 manual test — playing from the line you clicked

**Why this document exists.** The arithmetic is unit-tested: the play head lands at the *start* of the
clicked line's first move, a motionless line leaves the position alone, and the two sync directions do not
close a loop (`tests/test_main_window.py::test_playback_is_not_dragged_back_by_the_cursor_it_moves` fails
without the guard). What no offscreen test can judge is whether the feature **reads as one gesture** —
whether the play head visibly lands where you pointed, and whether the transport following your cursor
around feels like help or like interference while you are reading code.

Read `m10.md` first if you have not; this extends the transport it introduced. About five minutes.

## Setup

```bash
.venv/bin/foursight-gui tests/fixtures/baseline_4axis.nc
```

---

## 1. The play head goes where you click

| Step | Expect |
|---|---|
| Click line 16, `N120 G1 X10.0 Y30.0` | The tool marker appears **at the start of that move**, not at its end and not on the next move |
| Look at the slider and readout | The handle has moved off zero; the readout names line 16 and a time part-way through the program |
| Press **Ctrl+Space** | Playback starts from there and walks *forward* — it does not jump back to the top of the file |

**The property being checked:** `start_of`, not `time_at`. If the marker sits at the far end of the move
you clicked, the seek is using the segment's end time and every playback would begin one move late.

## 2. Arrow-keying is not a fight

| Step | Expect |
|---|---|
| Click line 12, then hold ↓ down the program | The marker walks along the path, one move per line, keeping up |
| Stop on line 10, `N60 M8` | The marker **stays where it was**; the status bar says `line 10: no motion` |
| Click line 3, the comment | Same: nothing moves, and the reason is in the status bar |

**Judgement call to make here.** A motionless line deliberately leaves the play head alone rather than
guessing at a neighbouring move. If that reads as the feature having failed rather than as it declining,
say so — the fix would be a status-bar phrasing change, not a jump.

## 3. Playback is not dragged backwards by its own cursor

| Step | Expect |
|---|---|
| Press Ctrl+Space and let it run at 1× | The code scrolls past under the tool, smoothly and forwards only |
| Watch a long move (line 18, `N140 G1 X50.0 A180.0`) | The marker crosses the whole move once; it never restarts at the beginning of it |
| Switch the speed to 100× and play again | Still monotonic — no stutter, no stall, no rewind |

**This is the section that matters most.** Cursor → play head and play head → cursor are a loop, and if the
guard is not doing its job the symptom is exactly a player that crawls or freezes on the longest moves
while the position twitches. Anything other than smooth forward motion is a real defect.

## 4. It works from the picture and from the findings

| Step | Expect |
|---|---|
| Click a segment in the 3D view | The editor jumps to its line **and** the play head parks at the start of that line |
| Click a segment near the *end* of a long arc (open `tests/fixtures/arc_full_circle_ijk.nc`) | The play head parks at the start of the whole arc line, not at the segment you clicked |
| Open a file with findings and activate one in the diagnostics list (double-click, or Enter on it) | The editor jumps there and playing starts from that line |

The whole-line behaviour in row two is deliberate: it matches the highlight, which has covered the whole
line since M3. A play head that started mid-arc while the highlight covered all of it would be two
different answers to "where am I".

## 5. Scrubbing still owns the position while you scrub

| Step | Expect |
|---|---|
| Drag the handle to roughly the middle and drop it | The editor follows to that segment's line, as it always has |
| Look at the readout | The position is where you dropped it — **not** snapped back to the start of that line |
| Drag the handle back and forth during playback | It seeks; playback keeps running when you let go |

**The property being checked:** the guard is directional. Dropping a handle inside a long move must stay
inside it — if the position snaps to a line boundary, the scrubber's cursor move is seeking when it should
not be.
